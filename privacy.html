Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how StreamHeat Poster (the "App"), operated by Stream Heat, handles information.

Who uses the App

The App is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Stream Heat accounts to post videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.

Information the App accesses

  • Access tokens issued by YouTube (Google), Instagram (Meta) and TikTok when the owner authorizes the App for their own account.
  • Basic account information needed to post, such as the account ID and username.
  • The video files and captions the owner chooses to publish.

How information is used

Tokens and account IDs are used only to upload and publish the owner's own videos and to check that a post succeeded. The App does not read, collect, or store data about viewers, followers or other users.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never sold, shared or sent anywhere except to the platform they belong to. Video files may be held briefly on a temporary file host so a platform can fetch them; these copies are deleted automatically within hours.

Data deletion

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in their YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account settings, and delete the locally stored tokens. Because the App stores no data about other people, there is no other user data to delete. Questions can be sent via Instagram @streamheatshorts.

Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
